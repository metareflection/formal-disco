A `lemma` states a named logical fact, typically a universally quantified property over logic variables. WP discharges the lemma as its own goal, after which it becomes a hypothesis available to every other proof. Lemmas factor out reusable mathematical reasoning that the solvers might not otherwise find automatically.

```c
/*@
  lemma square_monotone:
    \forall integer a, b; 0 <= a <= b ==> a * a <= b * b;
*/
```
